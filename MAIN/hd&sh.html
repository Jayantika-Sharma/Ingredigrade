Safe
  • Water (Aqua) – Base solvent
  • Citric Acid – pH balancing agent
  • Sodium Chloride – Thickener
  • Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride – Conditioning polymer
Mild
  • Zinc Pyrithione – Anti-dandruff active; effective but not for long-term daily use
  • Cocamidopropyl Betaine – Mild surfactant
  • Dimethicone – Silicone for smooth feel; may cause buildup
  • Glycol Distearate – Pearlising and texture agent
Unsafe
  • Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS) – Harsh cleanser; can irritate scalp
  • Sodium Laureth Sulfate (SLES) – Strong surfactant; drying with frequent use
  • Fragrance (Parfum) – Common irritant; no scalp benefit
  • Preservatives – Phenoxyethanol, Sodium Benzoate (safe in limits, not ideal for sensitive scalp)
  • Artificial Colourants (CI dyes) – Cosmetic only, no benefit
Alternative
  • Sebamed Anti-Dandruff Shampoo – pH-balanced, gentle
  • Minimalist Anti-Dandruff Shampoo – Mild surfactants, focused actives
  • Scalpe Pro / Scalpe+ – Dermatologist-recommended
  • Ketoconazole Shampoo (weekly) – For medical dandruff
  • SLS-free Herbal Shampoos – For sensitive scalp
