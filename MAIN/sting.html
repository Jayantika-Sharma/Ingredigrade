Safe
  • Carbonated Water
    • Main base ingredient
    • Provides carbonation
  • Citric Acid (INS 330)
    • Acidity regulator
    • Safe within permitted limits
  • Sodium Citrate (INS 331)
    • pH stabiliser
    • Generally recognised as safe
  • B-Group Vitamins
    • Vitamin B3 (Niacinamide)
    • Vitamin B6 (Pyridoxine)
    • Vitamin B12 (Cyanocobalamin)
    • Safe in regulated quantities
Mild
  • Sugar
    • High added sugar content
    • May increase obesity and diabetes risk if consumed often
  • Caffeine
    • Provides temporary alertness
    • Excess intake may cause restlessness or sleep issues
  • Taurine
    • Amino acid commonly used in energy drinks
    • Safe in limits but unnecessary for regular intake
  • Inositol
    • Vitamin-like compound
    • Generally safe but offers limited benefit
Unsafe
  • Artificial / Nature-Identical Flavouring Substances
    • No nutritional value
    • Encourages artificial taste dependency
  • Permitted Synthetic Food Colours
    • Variant-dependent artificial colouring
    • Linked to behavioural concerns in children (some studies)
  • High Caffeine–Sugar Combination
    • May stress heart and nervous system
    • Risky for children, teens, and frequent use
  • Preservatives (if present)
    • Legally permitted within limits
    • Not recommended for daily intake
Alternative
  • Coconut Water – Natural electrolytes, no caffeine
  • Lemon Water (Nimbu Pani) – Light refreshment, low sugar
  • Buttermilk (Chaas) – Natural hydration and digestion support
  • Black Coffee (No Sugar) – Controlled caffeine without additives
  • Green Tea – Mild natural caffeine with antioxidants
