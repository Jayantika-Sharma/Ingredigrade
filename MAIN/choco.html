Safe
  • Whole Wheat – Source of carbohydrates and some fibre
  • Rice – Energy-providing grain
  • Cocoa Solids – Natural cocoa flavour
  • Added Vitamins – B1, B2, B3, B6, B12, Folic Acid
  • Iron – Fortification for micronutrient support
Mild
  • Sugar – Added sugar; should be limited
  • Malt Extract – Adds sweetness and flavour
  • Edible Vegetable Oil – Improves texture; excess not ideal
  • Salt – Low amount but contributes to sodium intake
Unsafe
  • High Sugar Content (overall) – Not suitable for daily consumption
  • Ultra-Processed Cereal – Low satiety, encourages over-eating
Alternative
  • Plain Oats – High fibre, no added sugar
  • Homemade Muesli – Nuts, seeds, dried fruits (controlled sugar)
  • Whole Wheat Flakes – Less processed
  • Poha / Upma – Traditional, more filling
  • Eggs + Fruit – High protein, natural nutrients
