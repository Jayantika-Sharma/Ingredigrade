Safe
  • Water (Aqua) – Base solvent
  • Glycerin – Humectant that attracts moisture
  • Vitamin E (Tocopheryl Acetate) – Antioxidant, skin conditioning
  • Cetearyl Alcohol – Fatty alcohol, safe and moisturizing
  • Glyceryl Stearate – Emulsifier and skin softener
Mild
  • Mineral Oil (Paraffinum Liquidum) – Occlusive moisturizer; can feel heavy
  • Isopropyl Palmitate – Improves spreadability; may clog pores for some
  • Dimethicone – Silicone for smooth feel; may cause buildup
  • Carbomer – Thickener, generally safe
  • Sodium Hydroxide – pH adjuster; safe in small amounts
Unsafe
  • Fragrance (Parfum) – Common cause of skin irritation and allergy
  • Preservatives
    • Phenoxyethanol
    • Ethylhexylglycerin
    • Safe in limits but not ideal for sensitive skin
Alternative
  • Cetaphil Moisturising Lotion – Fragrance-free, barrier-friendly
  • Simple Hydrating Lotion – Minimal ingredients
  • Physiogel Daily Moisture Therapy – Dermatologist-recommended
  • Pure Aloe Vera Gel – Light, fragrance-free hydration
  • Cold-Pressed Coconut Oil – Natural option for very dry skin
