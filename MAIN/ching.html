Safe
  • Water – Base ingredient
  • Garlic Paste – Natural flavouring
  • Spices & Condiments – Traditional ingredients
  • Natural Colour (Paprika Oleoresin – INS 160c) – Plant-derived colour
Mild
  • Soybean Oil – Adds fat; limit quantity
  • Sugar – Added sugar; excess not ideal
  • Salt – High sodium if overused
  • Acetic Acid (INS 260) – Acidity regulator
  • Xanthan Gum (INS 415) – Thickener, safe but unnecessary
Unsafe
  • Flavour Enhancers
    • MSG (INS 621)
    • Disodium Guanylate (INS 627)
    • Disodium Inosinate (INS 631)
    • No nutritional value; frequent intake discouraged
  • Preservative (Sodium Benzoate – INS 211)
    • Permitted by FSSAI
    • Not recommended for regular consumption
Alternative
  • Homemade Schezwan Sauce – Red chilli + garlic + oil
  • Fresh Chilli-Garlic Paste – No preservatives
  • Chilli Oil (Homemade) – Controlled salt and oil
  • Sautéed Garlic & Chilli – Clean, natural flavour
