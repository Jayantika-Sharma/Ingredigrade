Safe
  • Water (Aqua) – Base solvent
  • Panthenol (Pro-Vitamin B5) – Hair conditioning and moisture retention
  • Citric Acid – pH balancing agent
  • Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride – Conditioning polymer
Mild
  • Cocamidopropyl Betaine – Mild surfactant
  • Dimethicone – Silicone for smoothness; may cause buildup
  • Glycol Distearate – Texture and shine enhancer
  • Sodium Chloride – Thickener; excess may dry hair
Unsafe
  • Sodium Lauryl Sulfate (SLS) – Harsh cleanser; can irritate scalp
  • Sodium Laureth Sulfate (SLES) – Strong surfactant; drying with frequent use
  • Fragrance (Parfum) – Common irritant; no hair benefit
  • Preservatives
    • Phenoxyethanol
    • Sodium Benzoate
    • Safe in limits but not ideal for sensitive scalp
  • Artificial Colourants (CI dyes) – Cosmetic only, no benefit
Alternative
  • Dove Baby / Gentle Shampoo – Mild surfactants, low irritation
  • Minimalist Shampoo – Cleaner ingredient profile
  • Sebamed Shampoo – pH-balanced, scalp-friendly
  • WOW / SLS-Free Shampoos – Less harsh cleansing
  • Herbal Shampoos (SLS-free) – Better for sensitive scalp
